In this session, our Tenuous Trio is formed. A troll interrupts a good drink, a sketchy bard offers a quest, and a purple hippie offers an egg in these trying times.

## Characters

**Sweeper.** An Elven amnesiac. Quick-thinking and quick with his hands, this Elf awoke one day with only his wits and his name. He has since made a living through less-than scrupulous means.

**Aldertwig.** Wild Halfling in a big city. This nomad has somehow made her way into the City of Splendors. Don't confuse her naivety for weakness, she can punch you so hard that your brains will leak out your nose.

**Imogen.** A real Robin Hood. A human sorcerer that grew up on the streets of Waterdeep, Imogen uses her intrinsic magical prowess and sweet-talking to take from the rich and give to the poor.

## Who Did They Meet?

**Durnan.** Also known as Fred, Durnan is the owner and proprietor of the Yawning Portal. The warrior's grace that he carries himself with betrays the veneer of a barkeep that he wears - showing a glimpse of a previous life.

**Volo.** A widely traveled human wizard and sage of Faerûn. He is a legendary traveler, explorer, and rogueish mage, and is most famous, or infamous, for his guidebooks including the controversial and notorious Volo's Guide to All Things Magical.
